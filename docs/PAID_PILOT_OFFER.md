# CBOS Founding Clinic Pilot

## Offer

A 30-day controlled pilot for one chiropractic practice to test whether CBOS improves visibility and consistency for patient inquiry follow-up, reactivation, and weekly owner review.

**Pilot price: $100 paid before setup.**

The pilot uses fake data by default. Approved de-identified data may be used only after the real-data readiness gate is completed. Real patient data is not accepted during the initial pilot under the current readiness state.

## Included

- one configured CBOS pilot workspace;
- fake sample data or one approved de-identified CSV import;
- one 30-minute staff onboarding session;
- Patient Inquiries, Today action queue, Reactivations, and Weekly Review;
- one midpoint check-in;
- one end-of-pilot review using the success scorecard;
- export of the practice's approved pilot data.

## Not included

- EHR replacement or clinical records;
- SOAP notes, treatment plans, prescriptions, or charting;
- billing, insurance, claims, or payments;
- appointment creation or calendar replacement;
- guaranteed revenue, recovered patients, or time savings;
- custom EHR integration before export feasibility is validated.

## Practice commitment

- name one daily workflow owner and one decision maker;
- use the queue on at least 10 business days;
- hold one weekly owner review;
- record outcomes consistently;
- provide candid feedback and a continue / revise / stop decision.

## End-of-pilot decision

- **Continue:** workflow is useful, staff uses it, and the practice accepts `$99/month`.
- **Revise:** one bounded workflow or data issue needs correction and both parties agree to a short extension.
- **Stop:** the current system already solves the problem, staff will not use the queue, data cannot be maintained without double entry, or value is not demonstrated.

This is a validation offer, not a promise of clinical, financial, or operational outcomes.
