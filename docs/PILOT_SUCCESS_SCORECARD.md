## Outcome measures

Record, but do not guarantee or attribute causally:

- inquiries contacted;
- overdue items cleared;
- reactivation attempts;
- appointments reported as scheduled;
- opportunities marked lost or resolved;
- estimated opportunity value reviewed.

## Continue decision

Recommend continuing only when:

- the clinic names a real operational improvement;
- staff actually used the workflow;
- no unsafe data-handling issue occurred;
- the data path does not create unacceptable double entry;
- the decision maker agrees the ongoing price is justified.
