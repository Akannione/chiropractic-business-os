# Pricing Hypothesis

## Evidence available

One private-clinic respondent indicated willingness to pay approximately `$50-$100 per month`. This is one signal, not validated market pricing. Existing historical collateral with larger setup packages must not be treated as proof of willingness to pay.

## Models to test

| Model | Offer | What it tests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founding paid pilot | `$100` for 30 days, no long-term commitment | Whether the problem is valuable enough for a real purchase |
| Monthly software | `$99/month` after the pilot | Whether ongoing visibility and workflow support retention |
| Assisted setup | A separately quoted setup fee only after real import effort is known | Whether onboarding labor justifies services pricing |

Do not offer more than these three models during initial validation.

## Recommendation

Start with a **$100, 30-day Founding Clinic Pilot** using fake data or an approved de-identified export. If the practice continues, offer **$99/month** while founding-pilot support and product scope remain small.

The pilot fee should be paid before setup. It proves commercial intent without requiring an annual contract or an unvalidated implementation fee.

## What is included

- configured CBOS pilot workspace;
- fake-data or approved de-identified import;
- one staff onboarding session;
- daily action and reactivation workflow;
- weekly owner review;
- end-of-pilot scorecard and decision.

## Validation measures

- price accepted, negotiated, or rejected;
- exact objection;
- who controls the budget;
- setup hours required;
- weekly active use;
- decision to continue at `$99/month`.

## Guardrail

Do not claim guaranteed revenue, time savings, HIPAA compliance, or EHR integration. Any ROI example is an estimate for discussion.

Status: **YELLOW** until a clinic pays and completes the pilot.
