# Ideal Customer Profile Hypothesis

## Primary hypothesis

The strongest first customer is a solo or small chiropractic practice with 1-3 providers and a front desk team that already uses an EHR or practice-management system but still manages new-inquiry follow-up or patient reactivation through email, memory, manual reports, or spreadsheets.

## Observable fit signals

- 20 or more patient inquiries or reactivation opportunities per month;
- a website form, phone, referral, or marketing channels generating inquiries;
- no clear daily owner-visible follow-up queue;
- staff manually creates call lists or searches reports;
- missed or inconsistent reactivation follow-up is acknowledged;
- current system can export CSV or another structured report;
- one owner or manager can sponsor a 30-day pilot;
- team is willing to review a small queue daily and a summary weekly.

## Poor-fit signals

- buyer wants an EHR, clinical documentation, billing, claims, prescriptions, or scheduling replacement;
- existing system already gives staff a trusted daily action queue and measured follow-up workflow;
- no operational owner will use the system;
- practice will not permit fake or approved de-identified validation;
- value depends on copying the same data manually into two systems;
- enterprise procurement or deep integration is required before any test.

## Validation plan

Score the next 10 independent practices against the observable signals. Record the workflow, current tools, inquiry volume, owner, data path, price reaction, and Go/Revise/Stop result.

## Threshold

Keep this ICP when at least three of the first ten practices meet five or more fit signals and at least one accepts a paid pilot. Revise it if a different segment repeatedly shows clearer pain and lower onboarding friction.

Status: **YELLOW**. The hypothesis is grounded in current conversations but not yet supported by a paid customer.
