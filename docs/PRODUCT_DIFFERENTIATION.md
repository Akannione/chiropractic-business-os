# CBOS Product Differentiation

## Position in one sentence

CBOS is the daily action and practice-intelligence layer that sits beside a chiropractic practice's existing EHR, billing, scheduling, website, email, and spreadsheet tools.

It answers four operational questions:

1. What needs attention today?
2. Why does it matter?
3. Who owns the next action?
4. What happened after the action?

CBOS is not an EHR, clinical chart, SOAP-note system, billing system, insurance system, payment processor, or replacement scheduler.

## Why a practice would use it

| Existing tool | What it does well | Gap CBOS is testing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EHR / practice-management system | Clinical records, billing, appointments, patient history | A simple daily queue for inquiries, follow-up, and reactivation |
| Email inbox | Receives website inquiries | Ownership, due dates, status, and weekly visibility |
| Spreadsheet | Flexible manual tracking | Consistent workflow, urgency, duplicate checks, and owner review |
| Generic CRM | Broad sales automation | Chiropractic language, a small workflow, and faster onboarding |

The product wins only when it reduces missed operational work without asking staff to maintain the same information manually in two systems.

## 30-second comprehension test

Open the `Today` screen without explaining CBOS and ask:

> What do you think this system helps the practice do?

Pass when the reviewer independently mentions at least two of these ideas within 30 seconds:

- see what needs attention today;
- follow up with patient inquiries;
- reactivate patients who may be overdue to return;
- assign or complete a next action;
- review practice activity.

Fail when the reviewer primarily describes CBOS as an EHR, patient chart, calendar, billing product, reminder-only app, or generic CRM.

Record the exact first sentence the reviewer uses. Do not coach the answer.

## Current product evidence

- `Today` is the primary navigation destination and uses action-first language.
- The first panel identifies the highest-priority work.
- Overdue, due-today, ownership, status, and next actions are visible.
- Clinical, billing, insurance, and prescribing features are explicitly excluded.

## Evidence still required

- At least five first-time chiropractic staff or owners complete the comprehension test.
- At least four of five pass without explanation.
- No more than one primarily interprets CBOS as an EHR or scheduler.

Status: **YELLOW** until external comprehension testing meets the threshold.
