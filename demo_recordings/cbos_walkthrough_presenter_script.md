# CBOS Walkthrough Presenter Script

Use this when showing CBOS to a chiropractor, office manager, or front desk lead. Keep the tone simple: CBOS is a follow-up and reactivation layer, not a replacement for the practice's existing systems.

## 1. Dashboard

"CBOS gives the practice a simple view of patient inquiries, active patients, follow-ups, and estimated treatment value. The point of this first screen is speed: the owner or front desk should know what needs attention in under 30 seconds."

Point out:

- Estimated Treatment Value
- Follow-Ups Needed
- Active Patients
- Inquiry-to-Patient Rate
- Today's Follow-Up Workflow

## 2. Patient Inquiries

"When someone calls, fills out the website form, comes from Google, or is referred by another patient, staff can enter the inquiry here. The goal is to stop relying on memory, scattered emails, or handwritten notes."

Point out:

- patient contact details
- requested service
- inquiry source
- status
- next follow-up date
- activity or movement context

## 3. Follow-Up Workflow

"This makes follow-up visible. Staff can see who needs a call today, who is overdue, and who moved forward to consultation or active patient status."

If they ask who enters information:

"Usually the front desk enters it, but the public intake link can also be connected to the website, Google Business Profile, or referral partners."

## 4. Reactivations

"This is the gap many clinics describe: patients who should come back weekly or monthly are not always easy to see. CBOS creates a focused call list for overdue, due-today, and upcoming reactivations without replacing MetaSoft, ChiroTouch, scheduling, billing, or the EHR."

Point out:

- overdue patients first
- due-today patients
- last visit date
- expected visit frequency
- follow-up owner
- follow-up outcome

## 5. Public Intake

"The public intake form can be linked from the practice website, Google Business Profile, or referral partners. The source can be tracked automatically, so the practice can see where inquiries are coming from."

## 6. Weekly Summary

"The weekly summary gives the owner a simple review of inquiry activity, follow-ups, active patients, and estimated treatment value. It is for a quick weekly practice review, not a complicated report."

## 7. Exports

"The practice owns the data. Inquiries can be exported to CSV, and existing spreadsheet exports can be previewed before import. That helps reduce migration risk because duplicates and bad dates can be caught before anything is added."

## Close

"The main value is simple: every inquiry in one place, every follow-up visible, and a weekly view of whether interest is turning into active patients."

Ask:

"Would this make today's follow-up work easier for your office to see?"
